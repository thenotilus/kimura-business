The Concept

Business, Martial Arts & Shared Experience

Business Connect is an exclusive retreat gathering ambitious professionals, entrepreneurs, and decision-makers. The experience is designed to create the ideal environment for meaningful connections.

Training is a powerful medium to connect and experience the mindset of discipline, focus, and performance. More than a traditional business event or a training camp, Business Connect offers an immersive format where conversations arise naturally from shared experiences.

One Experience, Two Tracks

Choose your primary discipline: Kickboxing or Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu. Technical sessions are separate, while all networking and business moments remain shared.

Selective Networking

The retreat is built on a foundation of curated exchanges and strategic networking moments in a premium setting, fostering authentic and lasting relationships.

Who are we?

Business Connect is created by Kimura Camp, a company specializing in organizing premium martial arts retreats since 2024.

With over 10 retreats organized in Ibiza, France, and Morocco, we've seen a growing interest in connections that go beyond sports. Business Connect was born from this demand: sharing ideas and business opportunities within a high-performance community.
